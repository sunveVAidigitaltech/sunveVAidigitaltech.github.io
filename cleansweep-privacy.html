Privacy Policy — Clean Sweep

Last updated: November 4, 2026

Clean Sweep is developed and operated by V AI Digital and Tech Services LLC ("we", "us", or "our"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring your personal information and files remain strictly confidential.

1. Zero Data Collection Policy

Clean Sweep is built with a Privacy-First Architecture:

  • We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data, usage data, or file system contents.
  • All scanning, disk analysis, duplicate detection, cache cleaning, and system optimizations take place entirely on your local Mac.
  • Your file paths, folder names, documents, photos, and system caches never leave your computer.

2. File Access & macOS Sandbox Permissions

Clean Sweep operates within the standard macOS App Sandbox security model:

  • The app only accesses directories and files necessary to perform disk cleaning, junk scanning, and leftover removal.
  • Any file deletion or moving to Trash requires explicit user action.
  • The app does not read the contents of your personal files, documents, or photos—it only evaluates file sizes, modification timestamps, and cryptographic hashes locally to detect duplicates.

3. Network Usage

Clean Sweep functions completely offline without an internet connection for all cleaning and optimization tasks. Network access is used solely for:

  • Connecting to secure payment providers (Stripe / Mac App Store) if you choose to upgrade to a license or verify purchase status.
  • Zero analytics, tracking pixels, or diagnostic telemetry are transmitted over the network.

4. Third-Party Services & Advertising

Clean Sweep contains no third-party advertising SDKs, tracking frameworks, or analytics services. We do not sell or monetize your data under any circumstances.

5. Children's Privacy

Clean Sweep is a general system utility that does not collect personal data from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

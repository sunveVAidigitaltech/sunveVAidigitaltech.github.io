Privacy Policy - GenZ Lingo

Last Updated: October 2026 • Official Policy

VAI Digital Tech ("we", "us", or "our") built GenZ Lingo: Slang Dictionary & Translator as a free, ad-supported mobile application.

1. Information We Do NOT Collect

GenZ Lingo is built for fun, discovery, and entertainment. We do not require accounts, logins, or personal details. The texts, sentences, or slang terms you search and translate are processed locally on your device and are never stored or logged on our servers.

2. Advertising & Analytics (Google AdMob)

GenZ Lingo uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may process non-personal diagnostic data, anonymous device identifiers (such as Android Advertising ID), and coarse location to serve contextual and relevant ads according to Google Play Developer Program policies.

3. Children's Privacy

We do not knowingly collect any personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. The app is designed for family-safe, teen, and young adult audiences.

4. Contact Us

If you have any questions or feedback, please contact us at support@vaidigitaltech.com.

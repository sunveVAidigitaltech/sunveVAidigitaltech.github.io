Privacy Policy - Auto Redial

Last Updated: October 2026 • Official Policy

VAI Digital Tech ("we", "us", or "our") built Auto Redial as an ad-supported application. This service is provided at no cost and is intended for use as is.

1. Information Collection & Permissions

Auto Redial operates locally on your mobile device. To perform its core features, the app requests standard Android system permissions:

  • Phone Calling & Phone State: Initiates phone calls to your designated numbers and detects when a call has connected or completed. We do not record or monitor your audio conversations.
  • Contacts (Optional): Allows you to pick numbers directly from your address book. Your contacts never leave your device.
  • Overlay / System Alert Window: Displays the floating redial control panel while dialing.
  • Notifications & Alarms: Schedules automatic redial tasks and alerts you of dialing status.

2. Local Data Storage (Call History)

Auto Redial maintains a local log of numbers dialed, call duration, and retry attempts strictly inside an encrypted local database on your device (Android Room). This call history never leaves your device and is never uploaded to any remote server.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Auto Redial displays ads via Google AdMob to support free usage. Google AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as the Google Advertising ID), coarse location, and diagnostic metrics to deliver and measure ads in compliance with Google Play Developer policies.

4. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this policy or the app, please reach out to us at support@vaidigitaltech.com.

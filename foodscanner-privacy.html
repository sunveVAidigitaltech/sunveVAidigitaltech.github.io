Privacy Policy - AI Food Ingredient Scanner

Last Updated: October 2026 • Official Policy

VAI Digital Tech ("we", "us", or "our") built AI Food Ingredient Scanner as an ad-supported nutritional analysis tool to empower consumers to make informed dietary choices.

1. Camera & Image Permissions

The app requires camera access solely to scan product ingredient lists, barcodes, and nutritional panels in real time. Scanned images are processed on your device or via secure OCR APIs to parse text into food ingredients. We do not store, archive, or sell your camera photos.

2. Health & Dietary Disclaimer

AI Food Ingredient Scanner is designed for educational and informational purposes only. It is not intended to replace professional medical diagnosis, allergy testing, or nutritional treatment. Always review physical labels and consult a medical professional if you have severe life-threatening allergies.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

We use Google AdMob to deliver banner, interstitial, or rewarded ads within the app. AdMob collects non-personal technical data including advertising identifiers, app performance diagnostics, and approximate location to measure and serve relevant ads.

4. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns, please reach us at support@vaidigitaltech.com.
